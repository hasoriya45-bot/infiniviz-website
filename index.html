01 — About Infiniviz

We give unbuilt architecture its first sense of light, material and place.

INFINIVIZ transforms architectural ideas into vivid, considered imagery, helping studios, builders and clients experience a space before it is built.

We combine an architect's eye with cinematic lighting, accurate materials and real-time technology, from the first massing model to the final frame, so people can see, feel and explore a design rather than only imagine it.

05 — Our Process

Clear at every stage.

A focused collaboration from first brief to final frame, with clear review points so there are no surprises.

  1. 01

    Concept

    We review your drawings, references and goals, then agree on views, mood, deliverables and timeline.

  2. 02

    3D Modeling

    We build an accurate model from your CAD, BIM or sketches, including context, landscape and furniture.

  3. 03

    Materials & Lighting

    Physically based materials, daylight studies and lighting design set the atmosphere of every view.

  4. 04

    Rendering

    Draft renders for your feedback, then high-resolution final frames, animation or real-time builds.

  5. 05

    Final Delivery

    Retouched images, edited films or deployed web experiences, delivered in the formats you need.
